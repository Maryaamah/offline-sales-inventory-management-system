
# Offline Sales & Inventory Management System

##  Project Overview

The Offline Sales & Inventory Management System is a desktop application developed to help businesses manage their daily sales and inventory operations efficiently without requiring an internet connection.

The system provides a centralised platform for handling products, customers, suppliers, purchases, sales and business reports.

##  The Problem

Managing sales, inventory and business records manually can be time-consuming and prone to errors. Businesses also need reliable software that can operate without depending on a constant internet connection.

##  The Solution

I developed an offline desktop management system designed to simplify business operations, improve record keeping and make important business information easier to manage.

##  Key Features

- Product and category management
- Customer and supplier management
- Sales and point-of-sale (POS) management
- Purchase and stock management
- Returns management
- Serial number and IMEI tracking
- Receivables and payables management
- Business reports
- Receipt generation and printing
- Local database storage
- Data backup and restoration

##  Technologies Used

- React
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Tauri
- SQLite

##  Application Design

- Desktop application
- Offline-first functionality
- Local database storage
- Designed for Windows deployment
